Story Scenes

The Seven Scenes

Detailed explanations of each scene — replicated as built-in charts so the story is readable even without Tableau connected.

01

Evolution of the Olympic Games

The modern Olympic Games began in Athens in 1896 with just 14 nations and 241 athletes competing in 43 events. Over the next 124 years the Games grew to include 200+ nations, 10,000+ athletes and 339 events at Tokyo 2020. This scene traces that remarkable growth through athlete counts, nation participation and event volumes year by year.

Key Finding: Total athlete participation increased by over 4,000% between 1896 and 2020.
Medals Awarded per Olympic Games
02

Top Performing Countries

The USA has dominated the overall Olympic medal tally, followed by the Soviet Union / Russia, Germany, and Great Britain. This scene uses a ranked bar chart to compare cumulative medal totals, with breakdowns into Gold, Silver and Bronze.

Key Finding: The USA, Russia and Germany collectively account for the largest share of all Olympic medals in history.
Top 10 Nations — Total Medals
03

Medal Distribution

Every Olympic event awards exactly one Gold, one Silver and one Bronze. This scene maps medal distribution globally, highlighting which regions dominate and which are emerging as competitive forces on the world stage.

Key Finding: Europe and North America together account for over 65% of all historical Olympic medals.
Gold / Silver / Bronze Distribution
04

Growth of Women's Participation

Women were absent from the inaugural 1896 Games but have steadily gained ground — reaching near-parity at Tokyo 2020 where 48.8% of athletes were female. This scene uses a stacked area chart to show the year-by-year gender balance shift.

Key Finding: Female participation grew from 0% in 1896 to nearly 49% in 2020.
Male vs Female Athletes Over Time
05

Most Successful Sports

Athletics (Track & Field), Swimming and Gymnastics have historically produced the highest medal counts due to their large number of events. This scene compares total medals per sport with a horizontal bar chart.

Key Finding: Athletics, Swimming and Gymnastics together account for the majority of all Olympic medals awarded.
Medals by Sport (Horizontal Bar)
06

Historical Performance Trends

Geopolitical events such as World Wars, boycotts (1980, 1984) and the Cold War shaped Olympic participation. This scene layers key historical events onto the athlete participation timeline to contextualise performance dips and surges.

Key Finding: Boycotts in 1980 and 1984 caused measurable dips in both athlete count and nation representation.
Athletes Participating Over Time
07

Key Findings & Conclusion

USA Dominance

The United States has the highest cumulative medal count across the Summer Olympics.

Women's Rise

Female participation grew from zero in 1896 to near-parity by 2020 — one of sport's great social milestones.

Global Expansion

From 14 nations in 1896 to 200+ today, the Games have become the world's most inclusive sporting event.

Athletics Leads

Track & Field produces more Olympic medals than any other sport due to its large number of events.

Resilience

The Games survived two World Wars, Cold War boycotts, and a global pandemic to remain the pinnacle of sport.

Asia Rising

China, Japan and South Korea have grown into top-10 medal nations, reshaping the global performance landscape.
